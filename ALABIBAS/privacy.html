Privacy Policy

At ALABIBAS, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or placing an order, you agree to the practices described below.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our website, create an account, or place an order:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Your order details and purchase history.
  • Messages you send us through our contact form or email.
  • Basic technical data, such as your device, browser, and pages visited.

2How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Manage your account and provide customer support.
  • Handle returns, refunds, and exchanges.
  • Send order updates and, with your consent, news about new arrivals.
  • Improve our website, products, and services.
  • Protect against fraud and meet legal requirements.

3Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with trusted parties who help us run our store, such as:

  • Delivery and courier services, so your order reaches you.
  • Payment providers, to process your payment.
  • Website and email service providers.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

These parties may only use your information to provide their services to us.

4Payment Information

Payments are handled by secure third-party payment providers. ALABIBAS does not store your full card or payment account details on our website.

Please be careful when sharing payment information, and never send full card details to us by email or message.

5Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tools to keep items in your cart, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our site.

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Some features, such as the shopping cart, may not work properly without cookies.

6Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no website or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We keep your information only as long as needed to fulfill orders, provide support, and meet legal or accounting requirements.

7Your Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Ask to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where possible.
  • Unsubscribe from promotional messages at any time.

To make a request, contact us using the details in section 8.

8Contact Us & Updates

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at:

  • Store Name: ALABIBAS
  • Email: alabibas.apparel@gmail.com
  • Customer Support Hours: 8AM – 5PM

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its updated date.
