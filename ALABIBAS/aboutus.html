Who we are

Our Story

Est. 2026 Own the Look

ALABIBAS began with a simple idea: everyone deserves clothing that looks good, feels good, and stays within reach. From Davao City, we set out to build a modern online store for people who want style, comfort, and quality in the same piece.

Today we offer everyday tops and bottoms designed to mix, match, and last. Whether you are dressing up for a day out or keeping things relaxed, our collection is made to help you find a look that feels like you.

Our tagline says it best: Own the Look.

What drives us

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide stylish, comfortable, and high-quality clothing that is accessible to everyone, and to make shopping with us simple, honest, and enjoyable from the first click to the final fitting.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Filipino clothing brand that helps people express themselves with confidence, one look at a time, and to grow alongside the community that wears us.
