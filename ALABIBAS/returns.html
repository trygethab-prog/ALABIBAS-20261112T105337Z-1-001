Return & Refund Policy

At ALABIBAS, customer satisfaction is our priority. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help. Please read our Return & Refund Policy carefully before making a purchase.

1Return Eligibility

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order, provided that:

  • The item is unused, unworn, and in its original condition.
  • The item is returned with its original packaging, tags, and accessories, where applicable.
  • You provide proof of purchase, such as an order number or receipt.

2Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If you receive an item that is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

To help us resolve the issue quickly, please provide:

  • Your order number.
  • Clear photos or videos showing the problem.
  • A brief description of the issue.

After reviewing your request, we may offer a replacement, refund, or another appropriate resolution.

3Refund Process

Once your returned item has been received and inspected, we will notify you whether your refund has been approved.

  • If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. The time it takes for the funds to appear in your account may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Shipping fees are generally non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or applicable law requires otherwise.

4Return Shipping

Customers are generally responsible for return shipping costs when returning an item due to a change of mind or incorrect personal selection.

  • If the item is damaged, defective, or incorrectly delivered due to our error, we will cover reasonable return shipping costs or provide an appropriate return solution.
  • We recommend using a trackable shipping service to help ensure your returned item reaches us safely.

5Exchanges

If you wish to exchange an item for a different size, color, or product, please contact our customer support team.

  • Exchanges are subject to product availability and approval. If an exchange is unavailable, we may offer a refund where appropriate.

6Order Cancellations

Cancellation requests must be submitted before your order is processed or shipped. Once an order has been shipped, it may no longer be eligible for cancellation and may instead be subject to our return policy.

Please contact us as soon as possible if you wish to cancel an order.

7How to Request a Return or Refund

To initiate a return or refund:

  • Contact us at: alabibas.apparel@gmail.com
  • Include your order number and reason for the request.
  • Provide photos or other supporting information if necessary.
  • Wait for our instructions before shipping the item back.
  • Once the returned item has been inspected, we will notify you of the outcome.

Please do not send items to us without receiving return instructions.

8Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or need help with a return or refund, please contact us at:

  • Store Name: ALABIBAS
  • Email: alabibas.apparel@gmail.com
  • Customer Support Hours: 8AM – 5PM

We aim to respond to all customer inquiries as promptly as possible.
